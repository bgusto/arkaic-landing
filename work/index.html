Design campaigns, run for you.

We run design and analysis projects for teams using STAR-CCM+, Fluent, OpenFOAM or in-house codes. Given a simulation setup, problem specifications including objectives and constraints, and relevant data, our team delivers results and a workflow that continues to add value after the work concludes.

  1. 1

    A design campaign, end to end

    A parameterized case, the inputs and their ranges, the outputs that matter. We set up the study in Evolve, run it across as many machines as it needs, and hand back the optimum or the Pareto front with everything behind it.

    You send
    A case file and a list of parameters.
    You get
    The dataset as CGNS, the model as .surrogate.h5, the workflow to run again.
    Time
    Two to six weeks.
  2. 2

    Field analysis on runs you have

    Modal decompositions of transient fields, POD and DMD, with the modes, their energy and growth rates, and a reading of what they say about the design. Sensitivity analysis across an existing sweep.

    You send
    Field exports from past runs.
    You get
    The modes and spectra, and a written report.
    Time
    One to three weeks.
  3. 3

    Full-field surrogates

    From an archive of past runs, a model that predicts the whole field rather than a scalar, with a held-out error map that shows where to trust it.

    You send
    Fifty or more past runs: inputs and fields.
    You get
    The model, its error report, and a notebook that queries it.
    Time
    Three to eight weeks.

Start with a week.

Send us fifty runs you already have, inputs and results. A week later you get a surrogate trained on them, its fit report, and a call to walk through what it found. Fixed price, agreed up front, and no commitment past that week.

  1. 1

    A call

    Thirty minutes on the problem and the data you have. Pick a time.

  2. 2

    A scope in writing

    What we need from you, what you get back, the date and the price.

  3. 3

    The work, in your workspace

    It runs in an Evolve workspace that is yours. You see every run as it lands, and you keep the workspace when the project ends.
