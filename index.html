Arkaic is building an open platform to advance discovery science and engineering with advanced algorithms, distributed workflows, and trustworthy agentic orchestration.

01Evolve

Don’t just optimize, Evolve.

Evolve removes the barriers to high-performance computing and distributed workflows. Built to drive loops around long-running simulations, it specializes in large-scale multi-fidelity optimization, surrogate modeling, and the construction and tuning of full-field surrogates and learned turbulence closures. Its unique meta-learning engine uses a corpus of prior campaign data to turbocharge new campaigns. Agents can help craft, scale, and mend your workflows, making Evolve the most effective tool for getting your work done.

02Design databases

Build design databases you can trust.

Building uncertainty-aware design databases with Evolve is nearly effortless. Its data structures and modules were designed from the outset with interoperability in mind. Build a pipeline once, then add runs, add a fidelity, or swap a solver. Frictionless and auditable.

How Evolve does it

  • Surrogates provide fast inference throughout the design space.
  • Improve a surrogate with one command, using uncertainty-aware sampling.
  • Reports on each surrogate’s quality are generated automatically.
  • Trained surrogates are portable: download one as a .surrogate.h5 file.

03Efficient Optimization

High-fidelity results. Low-fidelity bill.

Optimization using high-fidelity computational fluid dynamics (CFD) or finite-element analysis (FEA) often takes hundreds of runs and millions of core-hours. Evolve uses multi-fidelity surrogates that blend coarse-grid or reduced-order results, reducing the time-to-solution and computational cost.

How Evolve does it

  • Multi-fidelity surrogates learn how your solver outputs relate.
  • Constraints, multiple objectives.
  • High-dimensional Bayesian optimization treats
  • Batched evaluations parallelize the workflow.

04Portable Workflows

One file, in the cloud or on your machine.

Every model Evolve trains is saved in one self-describing format that records its inputs and bounds, its outputs, its training data, and the fit. Models move between steps, and between the cloud and your workstation, without conversion.

  1. 1

    Train in the cloud

    Run sampling and training campaigns across many machines at once.

  2. 2

    Download

    Take the model with you as a .surrogate.h5 file, and its data as CSV.

  3. 3

    Refine locally

    Query and refine it with Python wrappers, then send it back to the cloud to scale up.

05Research

Optimizers that remember.

The most expensive optimization problems repeat within a domain: a new airfoil is not unlike the last hundred. We are researching cognitive Bayesian optimization, which turns past campaigns into a head start on the next, beginning with geometry-conditioned priors for families of airfoils. The goal is fewer high-fidelity runs on every new design.
