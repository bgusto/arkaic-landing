Computing Built for Discovery.

Arkaic is building the world's most advanced multi-fidelity surrogate modeling and optimization platform. We enable discovery science with state-of-the-art numerics, distributed computational workflows, and powerful agentic orchestration.

01The problem

First principles computing for all.

At one end are enterprise platforms: capable, and priced, licensed, and onboarded for large organizations. At the other are free tools that demand deep expertise before real work can begin, and leave the workflow in pieces.

Arkaic occupies the middle. Advanced tools without enterprise barriers. The point is not to make scientific computing simplistic. It is to remove the unnecessary obstacles to doing serious technical work.

  • Building for curious minds

  • Accessible by design

    Powerful simulation should not be gated by enterprise pricing or a steep learning curve. We build software that puts advanced methods within reach of more people.

  • Built for discovery

    From optimization today to fluid dynamics and learned surrogates tomorrow: an ecosystem of tools designed for exploration, experimentation, and research.

  • Open to science

    Transparent numerical methods you can read and understand, and a controlled-source model that lets academia, government, and industry take part.
