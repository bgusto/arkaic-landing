Questions

What exactly is billed?

Each run’s seconds on its machine, at the machine’s hourly price. Starting a machine, and the minutes it waits for the next run, are on us.

What happens when my credit runs out?

Runs already going finish. New runs wait until you add credit.

What happens to my files?

Your first 5 GB stay for as long as your account exists. Beyond that, storage is drawn from credit; after 90 days without credit, files beyond 5 GB may be deleted, and we email you first.

Can I use my own solver?

Yes. Run your own code on the toolchain, with the compilers and libraries it provides, or use a solver from the catalog.
