Arkaic is building a next-generation, open platform for discovery science and engineering. Its first product is Evolve.

→Team

Team

  • Brandon Gusto, PhD

    Founder & CEO

    Brandon is a former Navy civilian scientist at NUWC Newport and DoW SMART Scholar. He led projects in multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization and GPU-accelerated adaptive beamforming for sonar. He trained at FSU as a mechanical engineer, applied mathematician, and computational scientist, and his doctoral work advanced wavelet-based adaptive mesh refinement approaches in the context of compressible and reacting flows, as well as ML sub-grid scale modeling.

  • Toan Tran

    Founding Engineer

    Toan is a research software engineer building scientific tools from statistical packages to full-stack data systems. At FSU, he analyzed large-scale in-vivo neural recordings and built a computer-vision labeling pipeline to extract neural clusters of motor activity in mice. At the University of Michigan, he collaborated with Stan developers on a Bayesian data analysis package, streamlining MRP workflows with visual analytics and a domain-specific model compiler.
