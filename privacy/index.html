Privacy.

Effective October 7, 2026

This page covers arkaic.inc, the website of Arkaic Inc. (“we”). It does not cover Evolve, our product at evolve.arkaic.inc, which has its own terms.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join the waitlist.
  • Your name, email address, company, and chosen time, if you book a meeting.
  • Whatever you write to us, if you email us.

We set no cookies and run no analytics, ads, or trackers on this site. Fonts are served from our own domain, so nothing is sent to Google.

Why we use it

To tell you when Evolve is open to you, to schedule and hold the meetings you book, and to answer your messages. We do not send newsletters or marketing email, and we do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives the waitlist and meeting forms and passes them to us by email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs.

Each follows its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until we no longer need it for the reasons above.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, or to leave the waitlist. Write to inquiries@arkaic.inc and we will act within 30 days.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
