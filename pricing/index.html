Questions

What exactly is billed?

Each run’s seconds on its machine, at your plan’s hourly price. Starting a machine, and the minutes it waits for the next run, are on us.

What happens when my credit runs out?

Runs already going finish. New runs wait until you add credit.

What happens to my files?

Your plan’s included storage stays for as long as your account exists. Beyond that, storage is drawn from credit, and we email you before deleting anything.

Does included compute expire?

No, as long as you renew. What is left at the end of the year carries into the next.

Can I use my own solver?

Yes. Run your own code on the toolchain, with the compilers and libraries it provides, or use a solver from the catalog.

Is there an academic discount?

Yes, half price on the yearly plans. Write to inquiries@arkaic.inc from your university address.
